Quality & documentation

Qualification-ready information, clearly attributed

Our goal is to help technical and procurement teams identify which documents relate to Golden Ocean, which relate to the manufacturing partner and which require customer-side regulatory review.

Specifications

Product parameters and analytical requirements supplied for technical review.

COA support

Transport documentation

The supplied 2026 air-transport report classifies alginate oligosaccharide as not restricted under the referenced IATA criteria.

Certification records

Where manufacturing-site certifications are presented, they should be understood as belonging to the manufacturing partner unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Manufacturing-partner certifications

The existing Golden Ocean capsule website lists BRCGS, NSF, FDA facility registration, HALAL and a Non-GMO statement in relation to its manufacturing partner. Copies should be provided in the qualification pack where applicable.

Do not describe these certifications as certifications held by Golden Ocean Industrial unless a document explicitly identifies Golden Ocean as the certificate holder.

AOS documentation

The current AOS file set includes product brochures/catalog material, specifications and a 2026 air-transport identification/classification report. Additional commercial documents such as current COA, SDS, allergen statement and Canadian regulatory-positioning documents can be added later if you provide them.
