Alginate Oligosaccharide (AOS)

A marine-derived oligosaccharide produced from brown-algae alginate, positioned for B2B product development in functional foods, nutraceuticals and dietary supplement concepts.

Request a sample
Product overview

A marine oligosaccharide from brown algae.

AOS is composed of D-mannuronic acid and L-guluronic acid units. It is produced from brown algae through extraction, depolymerization, purification and drying.

The food-grade ingredient is a light-yellow to yellow powder with good water solubility, supplied to Canadian customers by Golden Ocean Industrial through its manufacturing partner.

Regulatory note: Customers are responsible for confirming the regulatory status of alginate oligosaccharide for their intended use and market, including in Canada.
