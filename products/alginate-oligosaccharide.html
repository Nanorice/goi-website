Alginate
Oligosaccharide
(AOS)

A marine-derived oligosaccharide produced from brown-algae alginate, positioned for B2B product development in functional foods, nutraceuticals and dietary supplement concepts.

Request a sampleDownload product catalog

New bioactive compound from brown algae.

DK-AOS is composed of D-mannuronic acid and L-guluronic acid units, with a degree of polymerization reported in the supplied product materials as 2–10. The manufacturing materials describe production from brown algae through extraction, hydrolysis/depolymerization, purification and drying.

The food-grade product materials describe the ingredient as a light yellow to yellow powder with good water solubility. Golden Ocean Industrial acts as Canadian distributor for the manufacturer, Qingdao Dakang Marine Biotechnology Co., Ltd.

Regulatory note: Product approvals and claims described in manufacturer materials relate to the referenced jurisdiction and supplied documentation. Canadian customers should complete their own regulatory and formulation review for intended use in Canada.

Designed for formulation work.

The supplied materials identify functional foods, nutraceuticals, dietary supplements and medicinal-food concepts, with examples including solid beverages, functional drinks, dairy products, energy bars and compressed candies.

Functional food

Suitable for ingredient evaluation in food and beverage product-development workflows.

Nutraceuticals

Can be assessed as a marine-derived ingredient for nutraceutical and supplement concepts.

Product formats

Manufacturer materials reference drinks, dairy products, energy bars, solid beverages and compressed candies.

Preclinical findings described by the manufacturer.

Manufacturer materials report animal-study findings involving gut microbiota, short-chain fatty acids, memory models and bowel-function models. These are preclinical findings and should not be presented as established human clinical outcomes.

Prebiotic research

Animal studies cited by the manufacturer report changes in selected next-generation probiotic abundance and short-chain fatty acid synthesis.

Memory models

Preclinical studies are described in normally aging and scopolamine-induced memory-impairment animal models.

Bowel-function models

Animal experiments cited in the supplied materials evaluate first-defecation time, intestinal propulsion and fecal water content.

Qualification first

Commercial use should be assessed against the customer’s regulatory, safety, formulation and labeling requirements.
